Jesus,
we pray, we hope,
and we surrender to Your Mighty Hand.

When the world trembles with anger,
teach our hearts to remain gentle.
When nations rise against nations,
teach humanity the weight of peace.

The poison of serpents wounds the flesh,
but the poison within the heart
can darken generations.

Yet still, Your light remains.

For vengeance is Yours, O Lord, not ours.
You alone carry the wisdom of judgment.
You alone see what mankind cannot see.

So we release our fear into Your hands.
We release our hatred into Your fire.
We release tomorrow into Your mercy.

And when humanity reaches its deepest night,
let Your dawn rise again upon the earth.

Hallelujah.
Amen.
